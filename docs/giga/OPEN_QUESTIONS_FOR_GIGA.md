# Open questions for Giga and implementing partners

*CC BY 4.0 · Proposal package for a school pilot · v0.7.0*

Decisions that only Giga, a ministry, a connectivity provider or another partner can take. None of them is assumed answered anywhere in this repository. Each is validated directly with the partner and recorded, with its source, before the stage that depends on it.

## Programme and scope

1. Is a pilot of accountable school filtering of interest to Giga at all, and in which programme context?
2. Which country, and which ministry unit, would be the counterpart?
3. Which schools (2–5 for the alpha) would be selected from Giga Maps, and on what criteria (connectivity type, size, language)?
4. Which school levels are in scope (primary, secondary), and should teachers have a separate profile?
5. Who at Giga would act as a governing signer, and is a Giga signature compatible with Giga's institutional role?
5a. Should the independent reviewer also be a governing signer, or should the third signer be a distinct institution?
5b. Do parents or the community need an appeal channel of their own?

## Governance and policy

6. Would the ministry accept a policy that is published in full, with reasons, and that expires unless renewed?
7. Which categories may never be blocked in the pilot (for example health, sexuality education, news)? This goes into the scope charter.
8. Who is the independent reviewer, and how is its independence from the ministry and the operator assured?
9. What is the escalation route for an appeal the school disagrees with, and is there a national body (ombudsperson, child-rights body) that should be involved?
10. What fallback should apply when a policy expires without renewal?

## Legal and data protection

11. Which national data-protection law and authority apply, and does the DPIA need to be filed or approved externally?
12. Are there data-residency requirements for the resolver hosting?
13. Does the ministry or provider already hold IWF membership or an equivalent national list, and under what terms can the operator use it?
14. Which national hotline should the block page link to for reporting illegal content?
14a. What is the legal basis for filtering in schools in the pilot country, and who runs the child-rights impact assessment?
14b. Who stores published appeal documents, for how long, and who can withdraw them?

## Connectivity and operations

15. Which provider connects the alpha schools, and can it redirect DNS and block external resolvers for those schools only?
16. What are the link types and typical latency at the alpha schools (fibre, mobile, satellite)?
17. Is there an existing protective DNS or filter in those schools that the pilot would replace or sit alongside?
18. Can the reviewer's test kit run on machines where Giga Meter is already installed?
19. Which hours and channels should operator support cover?

## Funding and ownership

20. Who would operate the system after the pilot (ministry, provider, regional host, or a contractor)?
21. Who would pay operating costs after the pilot, and through which procurement route?
22. Is phase 2 (20–50 schools) something Giga would consider, and what evidence would it need at Gate C?
22a. Who would fund the alpha itself (resolver hosting, reviewer, support during weeks 5–16)?

## Communication and languages

23. Which languages are needed for the block page, school guide and community notice?
24. Who approves the community notice, and is parental information required by national rules?
25. May the pilot, Giga's role and the country be named publicly, and when?
