# ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-09

## Context

The project will be reviewed by funders, the Digital Public Goods Alliance, auditors and partner institutions, and built by people who were not in the room when the design was discussed. They need to know why things are the way they are, not only what they are.

## Decision

Significant decisions are written as short ADRs in `docs/adr/`. A change to contracts, schemas, data formats, the privacy model or the core principles needs an ADR before code.

## Consequences

Some overhead per decision. In exchange, reviews are faster and decisions are not reopened by accident.
